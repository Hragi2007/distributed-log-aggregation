Distributed Log Aggregation with SDN Prioritisation

Project 10 · PES University · Goutami Velaga, Jahnavi S, Harshith Ragi

Under a 12 Mbps congestion load, critical logs averaged about 6 ms with the controller, against about 459 ms without it. Every other severity took about 455 ms in both runs. No logs were lost in either run.

Topology

Three clients send to one server. All traffic crosses the s1-s2 link, so that link is where congestion happens and where the queues are applied.

Limitations

  • Each condition was run once for the UDP logs, so the numbers show the effect but are not averaged over repeats.
  • Everything runs in emulation on one VM. The delays and bandwidths describe this setup, not a physical network.
  • Congestion comes from a 12 Mbps iperf stream. The log clients alone send too little to fill the link.
  • Network flow statistics are not collected by the controller. This part of the spreadsheet is not implemented.
